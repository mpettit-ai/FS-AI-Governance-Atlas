# Artificial Intelligence Acceptable Use Policy

**Audience:** all employees, contractors, and third parties acting on behalf of the bank.
**Owner:** AI Governance, with Information Security and Legal.
**Review:** annual, or on material regulatory or technology change.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for using artificial intelligence tools in the course of bank work. It is written to be read once and remembered, so it is short and it is specific.

## 2. The approved tools

The bank provides sanctioned AI tools. The current list is maintained at [internal location].

**Use the approved tools.** Do not use unapproved AI services for bank work, including free consumer versions of services the bank licenses, personal accounts, or browser extensions that send content to an AI service.

If an approved tool cannot do what you need, request the capability through AI Governance rather than working around it. The program's obligation in return is to answer quickly and to say why when the answer is no.

## 3. What never goes into an AI tool

Regardless of which tool, approved or not, do not enter:

- Customer names, account numbers, balances, transaction detail, or any other customer non-public personal information, unless the tool is explicitly approved for that data classification and you are using it within the approved use case
- Social security numbers, government identification numbers, or payment card numbers
- Credentials, API keys, tokens, or secrets of any kind
- Material non-public information
- Information subject to attorney-client privilege or covered by a litigation hold
- Information about an active investigation, including internal investigations and suspicious activity
- Personnel matters involving an identifiable employee
- Information the bank received under a confidentiality obligation to a third party

**When unsure, do not.** Ask AI Governance. This is a one-message question with a same-day answer.

## 4. How to use AI output

**You are responsible for anything you produce.** Output from an AI tool is a draft, not a source. The system can state something false with complete confidence, and that failure mode is normal rather than exceptional.

- Verify any fact, figure, rate, date, citation, policy statement, or legal reference before it leaves your hands
- Never send AI-generated content to a customer without reading and correcting it
- Never rely on AI output for a customer-facing statement about a fee, rate, term, or right without verifying it against the authoritative source
- Do not use AI output as the basis for a credit, account, employment, or risk decision unless the use case is approved for that purpose
- Do not represent AI-generated content as independently authored where authorship matters

## 5. Disclosure

Where you use AI in a way that a customer, a regulator, an auditor, or a counterparty would reasonably want to know about, say so. Specific disclosure requirements apply to customer-facing generative systems and are handled at the system level by AI Governance and Compliance. This clause covers everything else, including your own judgment about work product.

## 6. Before you build or buy

Any AI system used for bank work has to be registered before it is placed in production. That includes:

- Software you build
- Software you buy
- **AI features switched on inside software the bank already owns.** This is the one people miss. If a vendor enables an AI assistant in a product your team uses, that is a new AI system and it needs to be registered.

Registration for low-risk internal use is a short form and is usually same-day. See [`docs/05-intake-and-review.md`](../docs/05-intake-and-review.md).

## 7. If something goes wrong

Report it. An AI tool that produced a wrong answer someone relied on, exposed data it should not have, or behaved in a way you did not expect is worth reporting even if nothing bad happened.

**Reporting a near miss will not be held against you.** The program needs to know what is actually happening, and it cannot learn that from a population that only reports events with visible harm.

Report to [internal channel] or to any member of AI Governance.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations are handled under the bank's existing disciplinary and third-party management processes. The bank reserves the right to monitor use of AI services on bank systems and networks consistent with its acceptable use and monitoring policies.

The intent here is proportionate. Entering a customer account number into a consumer chatbot is a serious matter. Using an unapproved tool to draft an internal email is a conversation.

## 9. Questions

AI Governance owns this policy and answers questions about it. There is no such thing as a question that is too small to ask. The alternative is that people guess.
